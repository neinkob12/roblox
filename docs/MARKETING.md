# Marketing: Brainrot Cow Farm

Owner: B4. Everything here is captured from **real gameplay** using the trailer camera (type `/trailer` in chat, or press F7). Roblox rejects misleading thumbnails, and so do players.

## 0. Capture setup (do this once)

1. In Studio, press Play. Type `/cash 1e15`, then `/cows 18 1`, `/cows 17 3`, `/cows 15 5` and `/cows 12 9`. Step on the merge pad, so every stall shows a cow.
2. Type `/price 1.5` (the price board turns green) and `/truck` (the milk truck drives in).
3. Type `/trailer`. The HUD hides and the cinematic camera starts.
   - Keys `1`-`5` pick a shot: plot orbit, stall dolly, dairy orbit, top down, hero cow.
   - `[` and `]` change the speed. `C` hides your avatar. `H` hides the button panel. `F` shows the 9:16 safe frame.
4. For stills, use Studio's screenshot tool or Windows `Win+Shift+S` at a 1920x1080 window. For video, use OBS or the Roblox recorder (`Esc > Record > Record Video`) at 1080p60.
5. For **9:16 Shorts**, press `F`. Keep everything important inside the white frame and above the red bottom strip, where the Shorts UI sits. Crop to 1080x1920 in your editor.

## 1. Thumbnail recipe (16:9, 1920x1080)

The thumbnail is the most important search asset. It must read on a phone at about 300px wide.

- **Base:** a real screenshot from shot `5` (hero cow) of **La Vacca Saturno Saturnita**, the tier 18 cow. Make it **huge**: about 55-65% of the frame height, on the right two-thirds.
- **Text:** "MERGE BRAINROT COWS!" in 2 lines, top left.
  - Font: heavy rounded sans (FredokaOne or Luckiest Guy).
  - Colors: white fill, 10-14px black outline, yellow drop shadow.
  - Size: the text should be at least 12% of the image height. Use no more than 4 words per line.
- **Supporting elements (real gameplay):** the milk truck, a stack of cash popups ("+$1.2Qa"), and 3 small cows with a "+" between them (the merge formula) bottom left.
- **Colors:** saturated sky blue background, grass green, milk white, and gold accents. Push saturation +20% and contrast +10% in the editor. Do not recolor the models.
- **Readability test:** shrink the image to 320x180. If you cannot read the text or recognize the cow, simplify.
- **Don'ts:** no fake UI, no Robux symbols, no clickbait that is not in the game, and no other games' characters.

## 8. Brainrot name trademark check

Checked on 2026-11-07 via web search (news, Justia trademark mirrors, the UK IPO trade mark journal). **I could not query USPTO Trademark Search, EUIPO or WIPO directly from the cloud session**, so the human must re-check each name there before going public (search the original name AND our cow name).

**Context:** this is a live legal area. In 2025 and 2026, the Roblox game "Steal a Brainrot" (SpyderSammy / DoBig Studios) sued Mementum Lab over Tung Tung Tung Sahur, and Mementum counterclaimed for **trademark infringement**. Other trademark applications exist for TUNG TUNG TUNG SAHUR and TRALALERO TRALALA (Innovatex Labs LLC). Those two names are already banned in this game, and so is Bombardiro Crocodilo.

**Flags for the lead:**

1. **Rename tier 9 before launch.** "Ballerina Cappumoocina" is one syllable away from a mark with a pending Class 9 (games) filing. Suggestion: **"Mucca Ballerina Latte"** or **"Tiptoe Moochiato"** (keep the coffee-cup ballerina look; that is not protected by a word mark). Edit `src/shared/Config/Cows.luau` row 9 (lead-owned) and set `famous = false`.
2. **Tier 18 is the face of the marketing.** It is the least transformed name. Consider "Saturnomoo Saturnita" or similar, so the thumbnail and Shorts do not lean on an unaltered meme name. If it stays, re-check USPTO/EUIPO right before publishing.
3. "None found" is not clearance. The meme names keep getting filed. Re-run this check on USPTO Trademark Search, EUIPO eSearch and WIPO Global Brand Database the day before going public, and again before any paid ads.
4. Never use the original meme audio, images or the banned names in the store page, Shorts or ads.
